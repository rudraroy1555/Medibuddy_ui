How MediBuddy Works

CDSCO Standards & Generic Substitution Guide

1. Active Ingredient (API) Mapping

Brand names (like Lipitor, Augmentin, or Pan-D) are commercial trademarks owned by pharma manufacturers. Our search engine maps trade names to their standardized Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient (API) (such as Atorvastatin Calcium or Amoxicillin + Clavulanic Acid), revealing certified generic alternatives with identical chemical strength and therapeutic intent.

2. CDSCO Bioequivalence Standard

Under India's Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO) and Indian Pharmacopoeia norms, generic formulations must demonstrate bioequivalence within an accepted 80%–125% (90% Confidence Interval) geometric mean ratio for rate (Cmax) and extent (AUC) of systemic absorption relative to the innovator benchmark.

*Scientifically, claiming "100% match" is medically flawed due to normal biological variance, but CDSCO bioequivalence certifies equivalent clinical efficacy and safety.

3. Realistic Indian Market Savings

Innovator brand prices reflect patent development amortization and advertising. Certified generic equivalents from Indian manufacturers and PMBJP (Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana) Kendras provide equivalent therapeutic outcomes at 70% to 85% lower cost (e.g., generic Atorvastatin at ₹40 vs. brand Lipitor at ₹250).
