How MediBuddy Works

CDSCO Standards & Generic Substitution Guide

1. CDSCO Official Regulatory Database

Our primary dataset incorporates regulatory approvals from India's Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO). We map commercial brand names to their registered Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) and official therapeutic indications, referencing clinical bioequivalence standards (80%–125% 90% Confidence Interval).

2. Local In-Memory Search (Serverless & Private)

MediBuddy loads both the CDSCO registry and the 150 prescribed Indian drugs dataset into browser memory concurrently via Promise.all and PapaParse. Your medical queries are computed 100% client-side without sending search strings to any tracking server, guaranteeing zero latency and complete patient privacy.

3. Real-World Market Alternatives & Pricing

Prescribed brand prices often include innovator patent amortization and marketing costs. Certified generic equivalents from Indian pharma manufacturers and PMBJP Jan Aushadhi Kendras deliver identical chemical bioequivalence and clinical outcomes at 40% to 80% lower cost.

Clinical Advisory: This platform is designed solely as an educational discussion aid. Never alter, discontinue, or substitute prescription medications without the direct consultation and approval of your treating physician.
